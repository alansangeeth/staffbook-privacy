StaffBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

StaffBook helps you track attendance and pay for people who work for you. This policy explains what happens to the information in the app.

Information you enter

Names, roles, phone numbers, attendance, pay and payments are stored only on your phone. We have no server and no accounts, and we cannot see this information. You are responsible for keeping details about the people you employ private and for using them lawfully.

Backups you choose to make

  • File backup. Saves a copy to the place you choose. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Google Drive backup. Off unless you turn it on. Copies go to a folder in your own Google Drive, over a secure connection. The app can only reach the files it creates there.
  • Salary slips. Are sent only to the app you pick when you share one.

Android's automatic backup is turned off for this app.

Advertising

Unless you buy Remove ads, the app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location from that address, and information about ads and app use, to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and fix problems. Nothing you enter in the app is given to advertisers. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice under Settings > Ads. See how Google uses information. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Purchases and updates

Google Play handles the Remove ads purchase and app updates. We do not see your payment details. See Google's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet: for ads, Google Play and Drive backup.
  • Notifications: for the daily reminder, which is off unless you turn it on, and to tell you if the Drive backup stops.
  • Run at startup: to set the reminder again after a restart.
  • Biometrics: for app lock, if you turn it on. Android checks your fingerprint or face; the app never receives them.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it. Deleting a person removes their records, and uninstalling the app removes everything on the phone. Backups you made stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
